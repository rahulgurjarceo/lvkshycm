    About Yojana Connect Mitra

    Yojana Connect Mitra (YCM) is a citizen assistance platform that helps users apply for government schemes, certificates, jobs, scholarships, pensions and welfare services through official government portals.

    Become Agent / Partner

    Join Yojana Connect Mitra as an authorised agent or partner and earn by providing citizen services like Aadhaar, PAN, certificates, schemes, insurance, loans and more.
